# 09: Challenge and review

**When:** before sending results anywhere.

```
Re-test your own claims: (1) re-open every overlay and replot, recount markers; (2) recompute two metrics by hand from
the CSV files; (3) for each "agreement", show the baseline (BCS, EPW, inverted lambda) would not do as well; for each
"discrepancy", show it is not a definition, mu*, temperature or convergence artefact; (4) check that every grid,
smearing and mu* quoted comes from describe() or a log, not from a folder name; (5) list every assumption and what
changes if it is wrong; (6) correct anything wrong, with a dated note in REPORT.md.
```

**Check:** the answer lists what was re-tested and any correction made.
