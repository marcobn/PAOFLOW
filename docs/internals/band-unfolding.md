# Band Unfolding: Spectral Weights of Supercell States

This page derives the spectral weights that {py:func}`~PAOFLOW.models.band_unfold.unfold_bands` and {py:func}`~PAOFLOW.models.band_unfold.unfold_bands_sparse` compute. How to run them is in [How to unfold supercell bands onto the primitive Brillouin zone](../learn/howtos/band-unfolding.md) and [How to unfold the bands of large supercells with the sparse solver](../learn/howtos/unfold-large-supercells.md).

A supercell with lattice vectors $\mathbf{A}_i = \sum_j M_{ij}\mathbf{a}_j$, integer combinations of the primitive vectors $\mathbf{a}_j$, holds $N = |\det M|$ primitive cells, displaced from each other by the primitive lattice vectors $\mathbf{R}_\ell$, $\ell = 1, \dots, N$. Its Brillouin zone is $N$ times smaller than the primitive one, so $N$ wavevectors $\mathbf{k}$ of the primitive zone, which differ by reciprocal lattice vectors $\mathbf{G}$ of the supercell, fold onto each point of the supercell zone. The supercell Hamiltonian is the same matrix at all of them, and its $N$ times as many eigenstates are, in the perfect crystal, the primitive Bloch states of all $N$ wavevectors. Unfolding (V. Popescu and A. Zunger, Phys. Rev. B **85**, 085201 (2012); P. B. Allen, T. Berlijn, D. A. Casavant and J. M. Soler, Phys. Rev. B **87**, 085322 (2013)) assigns each supercell state $n$ the weight $w_n(\mathbf{k})$ with which it has primitive crystal momentum $\mathbf{k}$.

In an orthonormal atomic-orbital basis, as in the tight-binding models and the orthogonalised PAO Hamiltonian, the weight follows from a relabelling of the supercell orbitals. If every supercell atom sits at $\boldsymbol{\tau}_\alpha + \mathbf{R}_\ell$, a primitive atom $\alpha$ shifted by one of the translations, and carries the same orbitals $m$, each supercell orbital is labelled $(\alpha, m, \ell)$, and the eigenvector of state $n$ at $\mathbf{k}$ has coefficients $C^n_{\alpha m\ell}(\mathbf{k})$. The Bloch sum of orbital $(\alpha, m)$ with momentum $\mathbf{k}$, restricted to the supercell, is $N^{-1/2}\sum_\ell e^{2\pi i\,\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{R}_\ell}\,|\alpha m \ell\rangle$, with $\mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{R}_\ell$ in units of $2\pi$. The weight is the squared norm of the projection of state $n$ on these $n_\mathrm{awf}^\mathrm{PC}$ Bloch sums, where $n_\mathrm{awf}^\mathrm{PC}$ is the number of orbitals of the primitive cell:

$$
w_n(\mathbf{k}) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{\alpha}\sum_{m} \left| \sum_{\ell=1}^{N} e^{-2\pi i\, \mathbf{k}\cdot\mathbf{R}_\ell}\, C^{n}_{\alpha m \ell}(\mathbf{k}) \right|^2 .
$$

The sum over $\ell$ is a discrete Fourier transform over the $N$ copies of the primitive cell, which picks out the part of the state with crystal momentum $\mathbf{k}$ rather than one of the $N - 1$ other wavevectors that fold onto the same point. This is what {py:func}`~PAOFLOW.models.band_unfold.unfold_bands` evaluates: it finds $M$, the $\mathbf{R}_\ell$ and the atom at $\boldsymbol{\tau}_\alpha + \mathbf{R}_\ell$ from the two geometries, and diagonalises the supercell Hamiltonian at each $\mathbf{k}$ of a path in the primitive zone.

Three properties of the weights follow from this form.

1. **Probabilities.** The phases $N^{-1/2} e^{-2\pi i (\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{G})\cdot\mathbf{R}_\ell}$ for the $N$ folded wavevectors form a unitary matrix in $\ell$, so $\sum_{\mathbf{G}} w_n(\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{G}) = 1$ for every normalised state: $w_n(\mathbf{k})$ is the probability that state $n$ has primitive momentum $\mathbf{k}$. A state of the perfect supercell that is a primitive Bloch state has weight 1 at its own $\mathbf{k}$ and 0 at the others. A perturbation that breaks the primitive periodicity (a substitution, a change of on-site energies) mixes the folded states, and the weight of one primitive band is then shared among several supercell states.
2. **Sum rule.** Summed over all supercell states at fixed $\mathbf{k}$, the weights count the Bloch sums onto which they project: $\sum_n w_n(\mathbf{k}) = n_\mathrm{awf}^\mathrm{PC}$. A deviation means that the atom mapping or the k-path does not match the primitive cell. The sum rule holds only when every supercell state is computed; a solver that returns the states of an energy window gives exact weights for those states but no sum rule.
3. **Degenerate multiplets.** The weight is the expectation value of a projector, so within a degenerate multiplet it depends on the orthonormal basis that the eigensolver returns, which is arbitrary. Only the sum over the multiplet is defined; in the perfect supercell it equals the degeneracy of the primitive band at that $\mathbf{k}$.

The weights define the unfolded spectral function $A(\mathbf{k}, E) = \sum_n w_n(\mathbf{k})\,\delta(E - E_n(\mathbf{k}))$, the quantity to compare with primitive-cell bands and with angle-resolved photoemission. The derivation requires the atom mapping above: relaxed positions, vacancies, interstitials and twisted layers have no such mapping and are outside the method as implemented.
