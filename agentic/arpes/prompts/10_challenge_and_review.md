**When:** before sending results anywhere.

```
Re-test your own claims: (1) re-open every overlay (digitised points and the extracted dispersion on the map);
(2) recompute two metrics by hand from the CSV files; (3) for each "agreement", show the baseline would not do as well;
for each "discrepancy", show it is not a bare-band, alignment, resolution or ingestion artefact (change the window and
the method, and report the spread of lambda); (4) list every assumption and what changes if it is wrong; (5) correct
anything wrong, with a dated note in REPORT.md.
```

**Check:** the answer lists what was re-tested, the spread of λ with the protocol, and any correction made.
