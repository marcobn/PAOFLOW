# 10: Challenge and review

**When:** before sending results anywhere.

```
Re-test your own claims: (1) re-open every overlay and replot, recount markers; (2) recompute two metrics by hand from
the CSV files; (3) for each "agreement", show the baseline would not do as well; for each "discrepancy", show it is not
an observable mismatch (drift/Hall, rho0, doping), an ingestion or a condition artefact; (4) check that the flags of
every run quoted (long-range, quadrupoles, screening, smearing, grids, channels) come from its log or metadata, not
from its folder name; (5) list every assumption and what changes if it is wrong; (6) correct anything wrong, with a
dated note in REPORT.md.
```

**Check:** the answer lists what was re-tested and any correction made.
