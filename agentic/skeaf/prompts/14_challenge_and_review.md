# 14: Challenge the agent's claims

```
Re-test the following statement quantitatively before answering: "<statement>".
Give the test, the numbers with their files, the chance baseline, and whether the statement survives,
must be weakened, or is wrong. If wrong or too strong, propose the corrected wording and update the
affected reports/README with a dated note. List any other claim in the reports that rests on the same evidence.
```

Useful targets: "X is reproduced", "Y is new", "Z is converged", "breakdown is active", "the shift improves the fit".
